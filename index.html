Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a listing?
Go to the "Add Listing" tab, fill in property details, upload photos, and submit for review.
How do I book a property?
Browse listings, select your desired property, choose dates, and complete the booking process.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept credit cards, debit cards, and digital wallet payments through our secure payment system.
How do I cancel a booking?
Go to your bookings, select the reservation, and follow the cancellation process. Refund policies vary by property.
How do I contact a property owner?
Use the in-app messaging feature to communicate directly with property owners.
Is my personal information secure?
Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
